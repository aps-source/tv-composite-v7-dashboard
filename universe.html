Rules & caveats

Same rules as the live engine, nothing re-tuned for stocks: Hourly EMA5>26>50 stack + 15m stack + Daily CPR TC, entry bar volume > 1.2× its 20-bar average with a strong candle; blocked on wide-CPR days, 10:xx and 15:xx bars, after 2 losses/day per symbol and on high/spiking India VIX; exit after 2 closes beyond the 15m EMA5 or at end of day. P&L is in underlying terms (% of price, and points × lot) — a proxy for an ITM option, not real premium. The rules were fitted on NIFTY/SENSEX only, so the stocks are out-of-sample by symbol, but they share the same calendar months. Dhan's equity history ends at 15:14 on recent sessions (24 bars), so end-of-day exits there use the 15:00 bar's close (~15:15).
